Loan origination practices: the workflow covers application intake, identity and document collection, credit assessment, approval with defined authority levels, offer, disbursement and servicing. Decisions need a documented rationale and an audit trail; where automated scoring is used, its inputs and outputs should be explainable and reviewable by a human.

Loan origination practices: approval rules and credit policy change over time, so they should be configurable and versioned. Disbursement must be atomic with ledger postings and reconciled. Fair-treatment, disclosure and data-protection obligations vary by jurisdiction and must be confirmed with compliance.
